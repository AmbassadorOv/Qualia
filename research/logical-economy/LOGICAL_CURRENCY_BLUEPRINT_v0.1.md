# AI231 Logical Economy & Logical Currency — Open Blueprint v0.1

**Status:** Open research proposal. Not a launched currency, securities offering, or claim of international recognition.

## Governing objective
Increase logical value: demonstrable improvement in correctness, consistency, proof, reproducibility, interoperability, or useful problem-solving. Financial price, token demand, and publicity are separate economic signals; none proves logical validity.

## Logical Islands
A Logical Island is a bounded domain with declared vocabulary, scope, axioms, rules, constraints, assumptions, evidence, provenance, and testable interfaces. Cross-island exchanges must preserve source, version, permissions, dependencies, and verification status.

## Exchangeable assets and services
Candidate assets include formal definitions, verified mappings, proofs, counterexamples, reproducible tests, gate configurations, software modules, validators, audit tools, expert review, verification, translation, and integration services. Contributors must have the right to publish or license submitted material.

Submission states: SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW, VERIFIED, ACCEPTED, REJECTED, UNRESOLVED. Submission is not automatic validation.

## Proposed Logical Currency
Working name: LOGIC (provisional). Name, ticker, supply, denomination, blockchain, issuer, and legal classification are undecided.

The proposed role is a common unit of account and/or settlement for permitted exchanges between Logical Islands. It must not be described as having intrinsic value, guaranteed appreciation, guaranteed redemption, or international recognition.

### Proposed two-part lifecycle
1. Contribution recognition: record contributor attribution, rights/license, evidence, tests, dependencies, and review outcome.
2. Exchange circulation: islands may use the currency to pay for accepted assets or services under published terms.

Recognition does not itself mint a tradable token. Issuance, supply policy, reserves, redemption, or market operations require separate technical, economic, governance, and legal approval.

## Logical value is not market price
Report quality dimensions separately: validity, reproducibility, utility, interoperability, novelty/additionality, and maintenance. Do not collapse these into a single score until methodology, weights, anti-gaming controls, and independent validation are published. Popularity or token price is never a substitute for proof.

## Membership and equity are separate
The broader project has discussed baseline membership and additional equity recognition for valuable contributions. These are not implemented or promised by this blueprint. Company shares, membership rights, contribution credits, and payment tokens are distinct instruments. Equity requires corporate authorization, definitive documents, cap-table controls, eligibility rules, tax analysis, and securities-law review.

## Contribution record
Minimum fields: contribution_id, contributor_id (pseudonymous where appropriate), logical_island_id, asset_or_service_type, description, version, timestamp, source URI, content hash, authorship and license declaration, evidence, test suite, independent reviewer, decision, dependencies, conflicts, limitations, and any separately agreed compensation terms.

Public records must not expose personal data, credentials, confidential materials, or third-party IP without authorization.

## Governance and safeguards
Before issuance or exchange, publish issuer, governance authority, jurisdiction, legal classification, supply, issuance, burns, transfer restrictions, custody, redemption (if any), fees, conflicts controls, accounting, audit, cybersecurity, incident response, dispute resolution, and upgrade procedures. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review and meet applicable AML/CFT, sanctions, consumer-protection, tax, privacy, and financial-promotion requirements.

No public sale, investment solicitation, exchange listing, custody service, or promise of returns is authorized by this research document.

## Open questions
1. Should contribution recognition begin as non-transferable credits, a transferable utility/payment token, or remain separate?
2. What evidence makes a logical contribution eligible for recognition?
3. How should independent islands price verification and integration?
4. How can duplicate claims, plagiarism, collusion, and wash trading be detected?
5. Should issuance be capped, demand-based, service-backed, or avoided until a real market need is demonstrated?
6. What governance prevents capture by one island, issuer, or large holder?
7. Which benchmarks show that exchange increases logical capability rather than token activity?

## Proposed implementation sequence
- Phase A: registry and evidence schema only; no token.
- Phase B: non-monetary credits to test attribution and contribution accounting.
- Phase C: simulated marketplace using test data only.
- Phase D: independent legal, economic, governance, and security review.
- Phase E: separate go/no-go decision before any compliant pilot.

## Invitation
Researchers, engineers, economists, legal experts, standards bodies, and independent developers are invited to critique the blueprint, submit counterproposals, build simulations, identify failure modes, and publish reproducible results.

**Core principle:** Every economic mechanism must serve the creation, verification, preservation, and useful exchange of logical value. The currency is a tool; logic and evidence remain the standard.
